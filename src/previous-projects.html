Zwischentöne

Clara Twele & Galina Kolchina
09. – 30. September 2026

A temporary installation by Clara Twele and Galina Kolchina, at the intersection of design, art, and architecture – held together by a soundscape.

Every space pauses before it becomes something. Zwischentöne is that pause. A threshold between the visible and the fleeting, between inside and outside, between matter, sound, and imagination.

With each step, distance shifts. Perception shifts. Surfaces appear, then disappear again.

All objects shown can be purchased.
