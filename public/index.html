Studio Kolchina & Gordon

The Studio

Studio Kolchina & Gordon is an art studio and collaborative project based in Berlin, working at the intersection of art, design, and science. Built around the ongoing partnership between visual artist and curator Galina Kolchina and composer and musician Liam Gordon, the studio brings together brands and artists who share a common vision and sensibility.

The studio operates as a place of encounter: a rotating programme of artists and brands.
